## About the project

CircoLogicLab is a Python project focused on exploring Boolean logic through the interpretation and evaluation of logical expressions.

The goal is to turn digital logic concepts into a practical tool, allowing users to experiment with operations and understand how different operators combine within an expression.

## Concepts explored

- Boolean values and their representations.
- Logical operators such as NOT, AND, OR, and XOR.
- Operator precedence and parentheses.
- Parsing and evaluating logical expressions.

## Technologies

- **Python:** implementation of the logic and expression processing.

## Future development

The project could evolve into a visual interface for representing logic gates and making expression exploration more interactive.
